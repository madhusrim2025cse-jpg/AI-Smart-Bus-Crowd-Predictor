AI Smart Bus Crowd Predictor


📖 About the Project

AI Smart Bus Crowd Predictor is an Artificial Intelligence based Smart Transportation System developed to predict the crowd level in public buses before passengers travel. The system helps passengers avoid crowded buses by recommending an alternative bus with less crowd and showing the estimated arrival time.


🎯 Project Objectives

  • Predict bus crowd using Artificial Intelligence.
  • Reduce passenger waiting time.
  • Recommend an alternative bus.
  • Display next bus arrival time.
  • Improve passenger comfort.
  • Support Smart City transportation.
